Pick a mode

Five ways to play. Every fighter, on both sides, has 100 HP.

  • Story

    Three missions with a squad, a boss fight and a twist. Your choice changes the ending.

  • Horde

    No teams and no end. Everyone fights everyone. Rack up kills.

  • Free for all

    Every dog for itself. First to 20 kills wins.

  • Team battle

    Blue against Red, ten against nine. First team to 30 kills wins.

  • Gun game

    Every kill gives you a new gun. Finish all ten guns first.
